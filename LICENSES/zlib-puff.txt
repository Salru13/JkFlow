(launcher src/bl_inflate.c, linked into sce_module/relocated_wine_app.prx) follows
puff's structure: bits(), stored(), construct(), decode(), codes(), fixed() and
dynamic(), with the same MAXBITS/MAXLCODES/MAXDCODES/FIXLCODES limits.
