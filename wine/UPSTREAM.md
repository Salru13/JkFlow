The Wine PS4 runtime of JkFlow 1.00 is built from three public source trees.
The patches in `patches/` apply to exactly these commits.

## The prospero-win Wine patch series

The Wine tree of the runtime is Wine `490f6d5` with 193 patches of the
prospero-win tree applied in the order of `patches/prospero-series.txt`
(`git apply`), taken from `wine/patches/` of prospero-win **after** our
prospero-win patch `0001` (it changes or adds 23 of the 193, among them 0734 and 0735).
`patches/wine/0001-runtime-tree.patch` then holds the rest of the runtime tree.
